Bundled NES box-front scans
--------------------------
Source: Video Game Museum, https://www.vgmuseum.com/scans/nes.html
Collection contributors: Aaron Mims, Dogmatix Man, Double Dragon Dojo, Eloist,
J. Soler, KaneRobot, NGA, TJ, Zone and Rey. Thank you for preserving these scans.
Per-file contributor assignments are not supplied by the source page.

or upscaling; converted to sRGB JPEG quality 85 with 4:4:4 chroma; metadata stripped.
Source URLs, original hashes and normalized hashes/dimensions are recorded in
nes_covers/index.json. These are box scans, not cartridge-label scans.

Source URLs and normalized file hashes: nes_covers/index.json.

Bundled SNES cartridge labels
----------------------------
All 109 SNES labels are JPEGs listed in the unified snes_labels/index.json catalog.
The earlier 48 comprise 42 user-supplied AI recreations and six composite-image
extracts. A newer batch replaces 58 PNGs with 43 AI recreations and 15 supplied
originals retained after the image tool rejected recreation, and adds three
user-supplied labels for Mega Man 10, Mega Man 12 and Mega Man 13. AI recreations
are not archival scans; lettering, illustration and small logos may differ from
references. Per-image source descriptions, URLs, hashes and dimensions remain in
the catalog. For AI recreations and composite extracts, historical scan URLs and
crop details are reference context, not independent verification of supplied image
pixels.

Published FAQ: https://www.vgmuseum.com/faq/faq.htm (checked 2026-10-28).
It permits credited website reuse and prohibits off-site image hotlinking; app
redistribution of media based on those sources is not expressly addressed. Bundled
at the project owner's direction, not represented as an additional license grant.
Original game artwork and trademarks remain their owners' property and are not
licensed under MIT.
Per-label source and matching notes: snes_labels/index.json and
CARTRIDGE-LABEL-SOURCES.txt in this asset bundle.

Original launcher code and author-created artwork (SVGs, backgrounds and templates)
are covered by the project's MIT license. Third-party cabinet images are not relicensed under MIT.

User-supplied cartridge scans, restorations and labels retain their original
provenance descriptions in the unified catalog. The new JPEG label bytes are copied
without modification; full-cartridge preview renders are not bundled. Source claims
are not independently authenticated; some labels have no source attribution. No
redistribution license is asserted. The Japan-folder Super Mario RPG print has
Spanish text and a USA product code, so its shell assignment is a user-selected
presentation variant. Bundling is at the user's direction, not a claim of
redistribution permission. Third-party game artwork and restorations are not
relicensed under MIT.

Modifications and generated artwork
----------------------------------
Retro Launcher processes some reference images into transparent, resized and
aligned cabinet illustrations; some conversion illustrations use donor images.
The project author also identifies their cabinet artwork as AI-generated.
The current packaged cabinet-to-ROM mappings are in arcade-metadata/cabinet-art.json.
The former source/review manifests are not retained in the current tree. Attribution
to an external source does not imply that every cabinet came from that source.
Generated/conversion illustrations are not certified photographs of authentic
factory cabinets. Processing or AI generation does not relicense external inputs.

See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for the retained rights caveat.

Gameplay-lit copies and Stacked fronts
------------------------------------
The current 381 Stacked fronts were supplied in retro_launcher_cabinets_v20/front
and imported on 2026-10-25, with backgrounds/resolutions unchanged.
379 PNGs were copied byte-for-byte. Batman and Simpsons were JPEG data with PNG
filenames; they were losslessly encoded as PNG with decoded pixel equality verified.
Creator, generation method and redistribution permissions were not supplied.
They are not certified historical photographs or established MIT-licensed media.
Per-file source-pack name, dimensions and hashes are in
arcade_presentations/index.json.

The superseded generated fronts combined illustrated upright cabinet bodies
with third-party gameplay stills, marquees and control panels. The following
source patterns/manifests describe those legacy inputs and retained lit copies,
not verified source material or gameplay for the current user-supplied fronts.
Screens are static artwork, not live games.

Source patterns (ArcadeItalia / progetto-SNAPS material and original rights holders):
https://adb.arcadeitalia.net/media/mame.current/ingames/{rom}.png
https://adb.arcadeitalia.net/media/mame.current/marquees/{rom}.png
https://adb.arcadeitalia.net/media/mame.current/cpanels/{rom}.png

The current index records supplied source-pack names and image hashes. Detailed
legacy download and processing records are not retained in the current tree.

Public availability and attribution are not permission to redistribute.
Game images/panel art and cabinet-photo derivatives are not relicensed under MIT.
Include these assets in the release rights review before public redistribution.
