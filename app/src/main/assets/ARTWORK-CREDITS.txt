Modifications and generated artwork
----------------------------------
Retro Launcher processes some reference images into transparent, resized and
aligned cabinet illustrations; some conversion illustrations use donor images.
The project author also identifies their cabinet artwork as AI-generated.
Per-file source/processing manifests in the source repository distinguish
documented external inputs from original/generated contributions. Attribution
to an external source does not imply that every cabinet came from that source.
Generated/conversion illustrations are not certified photographs of authentic
factory cabinets. Processing or AI generation does not relicense external inputs.
